    Tips & privacy
    • Say prices in English and end with “yen”, like “two thousand five hundred yen”.
    • Say “yen” right after the number, with a clear “y”. A pause before it, a slow “yen” or “en” is often missed.
    • While you speak, the number shows in a dashed box. If “yen” was missed, tap it to keep the price.
    • If it isn’t sure, it shows other possible prices. Tap the right one.
    • Tap ⌨︎ or the price to type it or fix it yourself.
    • Check the number before you say it.
    • Runs on your phone. No audio is uploaded or saved.
